choice hell

- do I use a `std::array` with a fixed capacity and then a top
  - I need to hanlde default contruction somehow => optional?
  - if I use a vector then am I abstracting away too much
- what sorts of push and pop do I need, am I overloading a little TOO much
- i initially wrote `push(T elem)` and `push(T&& elem)`
  - ambiguous and compile time failure for `push(42)` as both overloads can accept an rvalue and neither is preferred
- does perfect forwarding apply here? why not?

**learnings**

- if you want to support all types, just use a `const lval ref` and an `rval` overload
